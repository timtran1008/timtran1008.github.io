Quiz Game

How to play

  • Choose how many teams are playing, then press Play.
  • On your turn, pick a tile. Read the question out loud.
  • Tap an answer or say your answer out loud. Press Check.
  • If you answered out loud, press Okay for right or Oops for wrong. Then let the next team pick.
  • Some tiles give a bonus or take points away. Follow the message on the tile.
