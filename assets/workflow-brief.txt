THE WORK BRIEF
A practical worksheet from Tim Tran

1. THE TASK
What recurring task do you need to improve?
Who uses the output, and what do they use it for?

2. THE CURRENT WAY
What information do you start with?
What steps do you follow?
Where do you get stuck, repeat work, or make corrections?

3. THE STANDARD
Attach one example of a useful result.
What makes it useful? Name three things you can actually check.
What must a person review or decide?

4. THE CHANGE
Which part could AI help with?
What context would it need?
What should stay with a person?

5. THE TRIAL
Try it on one real task.
What improved? What needed correction?
What will you change before the next attempt?

6. THE CHECK
Does the result meet the standard?
Did total effort, including review, change?
Can you repeat the workflow on the next task?
