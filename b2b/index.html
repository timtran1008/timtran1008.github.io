ONE EXAMPLE OF THE SHIFT Take your monthly management meeting. The same shift works for any recurring work.

AI-ADAPT · THE MEETING TODAY

Everyone has AI.
The meeting runs the old way.

  • Each team prepares alone. No team sees what the others did, or where their work connects.
  • The head walks in blind and hears every report for the first time.
  • Everyone expects the head to ask sharp questions and give direction on the spot, so the hour goes to reporting.
7 DAYS
AI-FIRST · THE MEETING AS IT SHOULD RUN

The head reads first.
The meeting is for decisions.

  • Each team’s report lands in one shared source, connected to the other teams’ work.
  • The head reads it with AI before the meeting and sends questions and concerns ahead.
  • The meeting is only for solving problems and making decisions.
