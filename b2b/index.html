Most companies are AI-adapt: the tools arrived, and the work stayed the same. The return comes when the company goes AI-first and changes how the work itself runs.

ONE EXAMPLE OF THE SHIFT The weekly or monthly management meeting. The same shift applies to any recurring work in your company.

AI-ADAPT · THE MEETING TODAY

Everyone has AI.
The meeting runs the old way.

  • Each team prepares alone. No team sees what the others did, or where their work connects.
  • The head walks in blind and hears every report for the first time.
  • Everyone expects sharp questions and direction on the spot. The hour goes to reporting.
6 TOUCHPOINTS
AI-FIRST · THE MEETING AS IT SHOULD RUN

The head reads first.
The meeting is for decisions.

  • Each team’s report lands in one shared source, connected to the other teams’ work.
  • The head reads it with AI before the meeting and sends questions and concerns ahead.
  • The meeting is only for solving problems and making decisions.

What makes a strong first workflow

  • Recurring and visible. Runs weekly or monthly with a clear operational deliverable.
  • Named owner and reviewer. Someone owns running it after handover, and a reviewer knows what good looks like.
  • Approved tools and examples. Runs inside the AI stack your company already pays for and approves.
  • Measurable baseline. We can time a typical run today and count rework before we change anything.
