HOW THE TASKFORCE WORKS

A faster first draft helps one person. When the team still spends just as long collecting inputs, correcting figures, or getting sign-off, that is where we deploy.

1. Follow the work end to end

We sit with the people who do and review the work. We locate where time and quality are lost—whether the bottleneck sits in the tools, the instructions, or the handoffs between people.

2. Agree on a result you can measure

We select one high-friction workflow that matters to leadership and record the baseline: how long it takes to reach a review-ready draft and how many corrections a reviewer makes.

3. Build and test in your workspace

We deploy the workflow inside your company-approved AI tools using approved examples and data, then test it on real work with the people who will run and review it.

4. Hand over and verify the return

We hand over the setup, review checklist, and walkthrough to a named owner. Then we check back after rollout to verify adoption and compare results against the baseline—so you have hard evidence before expanding.

START WHERE THE REWORK HURTS MOST.

Most companies have five or ten workflows in mind. We deploy into one high-friction workflow first to prove measurable ROI, then expand across your other priorities.

What makes a strong first workflow

  • Recurring and visible. Runs weekly or monthly with a clear operational deliverable.
  • Named owner and reviewer. Someone owns running it after handover, and a reviewer knows what good looks like.
  • Approved tools and examples. Runs inside the AI stack your company already pays for and approves.
  • Measurable baseline. We can time a typical run today and count rework before we change anything.
