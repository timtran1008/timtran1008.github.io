AI SHOULD SAVE YOU TIME. SO WHY DOESN’T IT?

Sound familiar?

  • “I pay for AI, but I don’t know which parts of my job to give it.”
  • “Fixing the answer AI gives me takes as long as doing it myself.”
  • “I explain the same thing to AI in every new chat.”

Why it happens

You use AI like a search box.

A new chat, a quick prompt, a fast answer. So every chat starts from zero. Your AI doesn’t know your work, your standards or your files. It guesses, and you fix.

The new way

Onboard it like a new hire.

You wouldn’t re-brief a good assistant every morning. You show them once what the work is, what good looks like and where the files are. Then they get better every week. Your AI works the same way.

That onboarding is what I guide you through. See the two ways to start
